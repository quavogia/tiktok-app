Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our content-publishing tool ("the App") handles information when it is used to publish our own short-form videos to our own social media accounts, including TikTok via the TikTok Content Posting API.

1. Who we are

The App is a first-party, internal tool operated by the owner of the channels it publishes to. It is not a public service and is used only by the channel owner to publish content the owner produces.

2. Information we access

When you connect a TikTok account, with your explicit authorization, the App accesses:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): your TikTok username (display name) and avatar, shown on the review screen so you can confirm which account you are posting to.
  • Publishing capability (video.publish): permission to publish a video that you select directly to your authorized TikTok account.

The App does not collect data about any other TikTok users, does not scrape content, and does not access your followers, messages, or analytics beyond what is listed above.

3. How we use information

Information is used solely to: (a) display the connected account so you can verify it; and (b) publish a video that you have chosen, with the privacy level and interaction settings you select, to your own account. We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or resale.

4. Video content

The videos published are produced by us. The App transfers a finished video file from our own storage (Google Drive) to TikTok's servers using TikTok's official upload endpoint. We do not add watermarks, logos, or promotional overlays to the video before sending it to TikTok.

5. Data storage and retention

Access tokens issued by TikTok are stored securely in our self-hosted automation server and are used only to perform the publishing actions you authorize. We retain tokens only as long as needed to operate the integration; you can revoke access at any time from your TikTok account settings, which immediately stops the App's access.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell or share your information with third parties. Data is transmitted only between your device, our automation server, and TikTok's official API endpoints.

7. Security

We protect credentials using access-controlled storage and encrypted connections (HTTPS). Only the channel owner can access the App.

8. Your rights

You may disconnect the App and revoke its access to your TikTok account at any time via TikTok's "Manage app permissions" settings. Upon revocation, the App can no longer access your account.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children and is used only by the adult channel owner.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Updates will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
